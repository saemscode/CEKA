### **<mark>7. DATA PROTECTION, PRIVACY, AND HANDLING</mark>** 

#### **7.1 Legal Compliance** 

Both Parties commit to full compliance with: 

- **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019** and all regulations promulgated thereunder. 

- **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)** guidance notes and directives. 

- **IEBC regulations** governing voter registration data handling and electoral information dissemination. 

- **International data protection standards** including GDPR principles where applicable to platform architecture or partnerships. 

#### **7.2 Zero Personal Data Exchange Commitment** 

The Parties expressly agree that **no Personal Data** shall be purposefully collected, processed, or exchanged under this MoU. 

**CEKA's Nasaka IEBC** is intentionally architected with **zero-personal-data-capture** functionality: 

- No user accounts, registrations, or login requirements. 

- No collection of names, phone numbers, email addresses, or identification documents. 

- No tracking of individual user behavior beyond aggregated, anonymized analytics. 

- No cookies or persistent identifiers linking usage to specific individuals. 

**The Partner** commits to respecting this architecture by: 

- Not implementing unauthorized tracking mechanisms on CEKA platforms. 

- Not requesting Personal Data from Nasaka IEBC users as a condition of platform access. 

- Not linking Nasaka IEBC usage to the Partner's own databases or CRM systems without express written consent from CEKA. 

#### **7.3 Incidental Personal Data Protocols** 

If Personal Data is incidentally or unintentionally captured, discovered, or disclosed by either Party, the receiving or discovering Party must immediately: 

52. **Notify** the originating Party within **48 hours** of discovery via email to the designated focal point. 

53. **Quarantine the data** by isolating it from other systems and restricting access to minimum personnel. 

54. **Delete or return the data** within **7 calendar days** unless written agreement for retention is expressly provided.
