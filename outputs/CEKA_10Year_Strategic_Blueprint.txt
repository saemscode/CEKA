CIVIC EDUCATION KENYA (CEKA)
10-Year Strategic Blueprint  |  2024 – 2035
 

This Strategic Blueprint defines CEKA's organizational goals, growth targets, and development phases across a 10-year horizon. It guides annual priorities, funding strategy, partnership development, and product evolution.

Strategic Foundation
Mission
  To strengthen democracy by making civic knowledge accessible and actionable for all generations.
Vision
  A Kenya where everyone knows their rights, participates in the political process, and holds leaders accountable.
Philosophy
Faceless, leaderless, tribeless. Aluta Continua.

Goal Framework by Time Horizon
1-Year Goals (2026)
Finalize and stabilize core platform: Bill Tracker, learning modules, Community Portal, Civic News, People's Audit, SHAmbles
Achieve significant active user base growth across web and Android
Formal partnerships with at least 5 civil society organizations and universities
Secure initial grant or institutional funding through 2027
Full Swahili language implementation across core platform
Grow email subscriber base past 500 with sustained open rate above 40%
Apply to HRF Bitcoin Development Fund and AUCTF
Establish CEKA's Wikipedia article and formal media/academic citation trail

3-Year Goals (2027–2028)
Nationwide awareness of CEKA as Kenya's primary civic education and legislative tracking resource
Active usage representing all 47 counties
CEKA civic content integrated into at least 3 formal school curricula programs
Dholuo and Kikuyu content in active development alongside full Swahili platform
1 million Kenyans reached through CEKA-produced civic campaigns and content
Recognized institutional resource in Kenyan media and civil society
Financial sustainability via diversified grant and donation income
iOS app launched with significant combined Android/iOS install base

5-Year Goals (2028–2030)
CEKA referenced by Parliament, media, and schools as standard civic infrastructure
Thousands of citizen memoranda facilitated per Parliamentary session
Hundreds of verified volunteer contributors — translators, educators, developers
County-level governance tracking live across all 47 counties
AI civic assistant in active use — English and Swahili
Measurable improvement in civic engagement metrics in CEKA-active communities
People's Audit data cited regularly in Parliamentary and media reporting

10-Year Goals (2030–2035)
CEKA is foundational civic infrastructure in Kenya — as essential and trusted as a public library
Platform adapted and deployed in at least 2 additional African countries
African Civic Tech Alliance formed with CEKA's codebase as regional backbone
CEKA recognized internationally as a template for community-led civic technology
Self-sustaining financial model achieved
CEKA's open-source methodology published as a deployable blueprint for democracy-strengthening initiatives globally

  PHASE-BY-PHASE DEVELOPMENT PLAN  

Phase 1: Core Platform & Foundation — 2024–2026
Goal: Build and stabilize CEKA's civic infrastructure.
Launch CEKA App and website: Bill Tracker, learning modules, Community Portal, People's Audit, SHAmbles, Malpractice Report
Achieve substantial national campaign reach — Finance Bill 2026 full coverage
Establish organizational governance: formalize team, advisory council, contributor model
Secure initial funding; develop brand and communications standards
Lay groundwork for multilingual support: English/Swahili foundation
Publish NASAKA IEBC on Play Store and drive voter registration adoption
Implement platform security hardening: OTP rate limiting, Cloudflare, multi-remote Git
Phase 1 Outcome
CEKA is a recognized, trusted source for civic information. Foundational infrastructure — technical, organizational, financial — is stable and ready for national scaling.

Phase 2: National Civic Infrastructure — 2026–2028
Goal: Integrate CEKA into Kenya's civic ecosystem.
Expand formal partnerships with government agencies, education institutions, and mainstream media
Integrate CEKA content into school curricula via MOE partnerships
Roll out county-level features: local governance tracking, county budget explainers
Full platform maturity: offline access, advanced search, complete Swahili
Host national Civic Tech conference or hackathon
Establish CEKA as the default legislative tracker for Kenyan media and civil society
Join CTIN and contribute to the African Civic Tech Atlas
Phase 2 Outcome
CEKA is Kenya's default civic education and legislative monitoring resource. Institutional recognition from government, education, and media sectors. Mature contributor ecosystem at national scale.

Phase 3: Civic Participation Network — 2028–2031
Goal: Deepen citizen engagement and build a national civic community.
Build Civic Champions network — trained volunteers in every county
Foster localized content creation: county governance data, additional local language support
Integrate with civic data sources: Controller of Budget, county assembly portals, IEBC open data
Launch AI Civic Assistant for real-time citizen queries on laws and rights
Establish county data portals as national platform extension
Secure membership in Code for Africa network and AUCTF ecosystem
Phase 3 Outcome
A distributed civic participation ecosystem. CEKA empowers communities to engage at local and national levels. Kenya's civic engagement metrics show measurable improvement.

Phase 4: African Civic Infrastructure — 2031–2035
Goal: Scale CEKA's model beyond Kenya.
Adapt and deploy the CEKA platform for at least 2–3 additional African countries, beginning with East Africa pilots
Form the African Civic Tech Alliance — coalition using CEKA's open-source codebase as shared backbone
Establish CEKA as an international template for community-led civic technology
Develop sustainable revenue streams: civic tech consultancy, platform licensing for international civic organizations, social enterprise spin-offs
Publish CEKA's full methodology as an open-source civic infrastructure playbook for other nations
Phase 4 Outcome
CEKA evolves into a continental civic infrastructure brand. Financially self-sustaining. CEKA stands as definitive proof that community-led civic technology can deliver democratic impact at scale.

  FUNDING & FINANCIAL SOVEREIGNTY STRATEGY  

Diversified Funding Framework
CEKA's funding strategy prioritizes sovereignty — avoiding over-reliance on any single donor, government, or platform that could compromise independence.

Current Mechanisms
Ko-fi and M-Pesa public donations — community-backed direct support
Volunteer labor pool reducing operational costs
Open-source codebase on Cloudflare/Supabase/Vercel minimizing infrastructure costs
Grant Targets
HRF Bitcoin Development Fund — open-source civic infrastructure, Africa focus, censorship resistance alignment
African Union Civic Tech Fund — €15K grants, multiple rounds/year
Democracy-support organizations aligned with electoral cycles
Digital Defenders Partnership (DDP) — infrastructure security and incident response funding (application submitted)
Bitcoin & Lightning Network Integration
Accepting Bitcoin donations via Lightning Network provides censorship-resistant, low-fee, globally accessible funding aligned with CEKA's independence ethos.
Integrate BTCPay Server or Strike Lightning payment for donations
Publish a public Bitcoin donation address with on-chain transparent tracking
Hold a portion of the community treasury in self-custodied BTC multisig for operational resilience
Develop a 'Bitcoin for Civic Freedom' explainer series as both educational content and funding narrative
Phased approach: donations first → education content → full treasury integration → regional scaling
Long-Term Sustainability
Institutional sponsors and large-scale donors aligned with democratic strengthening goals
Civic tech consultancy: offer CEKA's methodology and platform stack to other African civic organizations
Platform licensing: CEKA's open-source model as a paid-support service for civic actors in other countries
Social enterprise spin-offs generating revenue while preserving civic mission
