CIVIC EDUCATION KENYA (CEKA)
Product Bible
 

This Product Bible defines every product in the CEKA ecosystem — purpose, user flows, capabilities, technical details, success metrics, and development roadmap. It is the authoritative reference for all product decisions, technical scoping, and feature prioritization.

  PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE  

Technical Foundation
All CEKA products share a unified technical backbone: Supabase (23+ Edge Functions, Row-Level Security, real-time subscriptions), React/TypeScript/Vite frontend, Cloudflare DNS/Workers/R2 infrastructure, Vercel deployment, and Capacitor for Android. The codebase is fully open-source at github.com/saemscodes/CEKA.
The platform's core design principle is layered civic engagement: each product corresponds to a stage in the civic journey from awareness through accountability. All tools are interlinked — NASAKA maps voter registration, the Bill Tracker monitors legislation, People's Audit exposes the fiscal picture, and Recall254 enables accountability when leaders fail.

  PRODUCT 2: LEGISLATIVE BILL TRACKER  

Purpose
Kenya's most citizen-accessible legislative tracker. Allows citizens to follow Parliamentary bills in real time, understand their implications, and take action via memorandum submissions. Differentiator is AI-powered plain language, clause-level analysis, and direct participation routing.
User Flow
User browses active bills by ministry category or search
Taps a bill to view overview, AI plain-language summary, clause analysis, and timeline
User follows a bill to receive push notifications on votes, amendments, and deadlines
User accesses the memorandum builder for formal submissions routed to Parliamentary committees
WhatsApp sharing of bill summary and submission link
Key Capabilities
AI-powered plain-language summarization of legal text
AI-identified Citizen Concerns per bill
Clause-level bill analysis with Finance Bill 2026 seed data
Real-time bill status and Parliamentary timeline tracking
Keyword search within bills and across the 527+ bill library
23-category ministry-aligned bill taxonomy
Sponsor extraction from bill PDFs
Memorandum builder: server-side deduplication, MP routing by constituency
Version comparison between bill drafts and amendments
Public participation deadline countdown
Competitive Differentiation
Compared to Mzalendo/Dokeza (institutional depth, Parliament endorsement) and the official Parliament tracker (raw data authority), CEKA's tracker leads on accessibility, AI integration, youth-facing content design, and its integration with broader civic tools. CEKA and Mzalendo are positioned as complementary rather than competing.
Success Metrics
Bills followed per user
Memoranda submissions facilitated per legislative session
Bill summary reads and shares
User engagement spikes during active Parliamentary sessions

  PRODUCT 7: NASAKA IEBC  

Purpose
An interactive geolocated mapping service enabling citizens to locate IEBC voter registration centers, electoral offices, and polling stations across Kenya's 47 counties. A primary tool for reducing friction in electoral participation.
URLs
nasakaiebc.civiceducationkenya.com | Google Play Store (Nasaka IEBC) | Wikidata Q139784812
Independence Statement
An independent civic platform. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Kenya or IEBC. Sources official IEBC documents supplemented by community-verified contributions.
Key Capabilities
Interactive map: 24,000+ IEBC centers across all 47 counties
Multi-provider geocoding pipeline (v10+, Titan AI cascade fallback)
GPS-enabled location search and turn-by-turn navigation
Community-verified data with user contribution pipeline
Operating hours, contact information, and landmark details per center
Multilingual support: 15 languages including Swahili and Sheng
Offline mode support
Privacy-first: location data processed locally, no personal data collected or stored
Voter registration deadline alerts
Traffic & Adoption
Peak traffic: 1M+ monthly requests requiring server infrastructure upgrade. Consistently high engagement during voter registration drives. Described by LinkedIn users as feeling 'Govt-level' reliable.
Success Metrics
Monthly requests
Play Store downloads and active installs
County coverage completeness
Community-verified updates per month

  PRODUCT 9: RECALL254  

Purpose
Civic education and mobilization around the constitutional right to recall non-performing elected leaders (Article 104, Constitution of Kenya). Most Kenyans are unaware this right exists; Recall254 exists to change that.
Key Capabilities
Interactive recall eligibility checker by representative
Step-by-step petition guidance aligned to constitutional requirements
Petition templates and legal text references
Active recall campaign bulletin
Links to constitutional provisions and legal resources

  FUTURE PRODUCT ROADMAP  

Next-Generation Products
Translation Bot
Automated translation pipeline for legislative summaries into Dholuo, Kikuyu, Kamba. Architecture designed across five tiers: Google Sheets zero-infrastructure through full Supabase-native pipeline with Telegram bot, Cloudflare Workers AI pre-drafts, and community glossary enforcement.
County Civic Monitors
Localized tools for each of Kenya's 47 counties to track county government performance, budgets, and local legislative proceedings, feeding into the national CEKA platform.
Civic Mini-Player
A persistent cross-site civic updates widget — a floating lightweight civic newsticker for embedded use in partner websites and media outlets. Modelled on a music player UI for cross-page continuity.
Offline Education Kits
Downloadable PDF and video packages for civic workshops in areas with limited internet connectivity, for use by community leaders, schools, and civil society.
AI Civic Assistant (Advanced)
Conversational AI capable of answering citizen questions about Kenyan laws and rights, drawing from CEKA's legislative database and Constitution content in English and Swahili.
