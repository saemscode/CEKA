The full fix
Before anything else: I found the actual reason bills disappear between "scraped" and "usable" and it isn't proxies. It's a dead conditional in your YAML. Walk through this with me because it changes the priority order of everything else.

The proof: two of your schedule gates never fire, ever
Your cron is a single line: '0 0,3,6,9,12,15,18,21 * * 1-5'. When GitHub Actions fires a scheduled run, github.event.schedule is set to that entire cron string, not the hour that actually triggered it. Your Flush Staging step checks contains(github.event.schedule, '0 6') and contains(github.event.schedule, '0 18'). Your Phase A/B steps check contains(github.event.schedule, '0 3') and '0 5'.

Count the actual space characters in '0 0,3,6,9,12,15,18,21 * * 1-5'. There are exactly four: after the minute field, after the hour-list, between the two asterisks, and before 1-5. Every hour past the first one is joined by a comma, not a space, because it's a comma-separated list inside one field. So "0 3", "0 5", "0 6", "0 18" cannot appear anywhere in that string, at any hour, on any day, ever. These four contains() checks are dead code. They evaluate false unconditionally.

Consequence: Flush Staging to Bills has never run automatically. Every bill your scraper successfully pushes to bills_staging sits there permanently unless someone manually runs sync_to_supabase.py --flush-staging by hand. Stage detection, corroboration, and deep relay all query the live bills table, so anything trapped in staging is functionally invisible to your entire downstream pipeline. This alone accounts for a huge share of "bills not being ingested after hours of running." They were ingested. Into a table nothing else reads from.

Same bug kills Phase A (Sovereign Refresh) and Phase B (Matrix Solvency) on the schedule trigger. They only ever ran when someone manually dispatched them with the checkbox ticked. Your 30-column NULL backlog has been growing untouched every week that nobody manually clicked "Run workflow."

Compare this to your news_check step, which computes the hour with date +%H in bash — that one works correctly, which is exactly why news sync fires on schedule and the other two don't. That's your template for the fix.
